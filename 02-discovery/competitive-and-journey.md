# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Priya, “The Overwhelmed Veteran” (UXR-01, UXR-12)
Role: A 34-year heavy viewer and 14-year subscriber who has watched the catalog grow while her ability to find something good has shrunk.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Goal: To pick something she’ll enjoy and start watching within minutes, so the evening feels relaxing instead of like a chore.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Friction: She opens the app, scrolls for about twenty minutes through 15,000 titles, closes it without watching anything, and goes back to a DVD.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** switching to a DVD
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Steps she takes
Opens StreamLine to find something to watch.
Scrolls for ~20 minutes through home rows. The rows are loud and new-skewed (UXR-09) and driven by an algorithm she can’t trust (UXR-10).
Finds “nothing I actually want” among 15,000 titles. With no mood or occasion browsing, and with search that only matches exact titles (BUG-1080), she has no way to narrow the field.
Closes the app without watching anything.
Reaches for a DVD from her own shelf. Her physical collection is the one source she trusts to be pre-selected.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Finds “nothing I actually want” among 15,000 titles. With no mood or occasion browsing, and with search that only matches exact titles (BUG-1080), she has no way to narrow the field.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “nothing I actually want”
